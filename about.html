INDEPENDENT PROJECT — 2026

A more human perspective on real estate.

MORA is an independent real estate concept by Caroline Wilhelm, bringing together thoughtful design, software development and an intuitive digital experience.

Created as a personal case study, the project explores a calmer way to discover homes: considered typography, clear navigation and a story behind every space.

I designed and developed the experience from concept to interface, combining visual direction, usability and front-end engineering.

Independent portfolio project. Properties, prices and imagery are illustrative. MORA is not a real estate brokerage.
